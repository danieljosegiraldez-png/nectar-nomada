**Assessment.** The package value `picking.selective_kg_cherry_person_day` = 50–80 is **below** every figure found. Costa Rica press puts a good picker at about 360 kg/day (derived), and the Colombian national average implies about 94 kg over an 8-hour day. Selective picking of Gesha at the start or end of the season can genuinely be low, so the floor may suit Panama microlot picking. Still, the range should be widened, kept at low confidence, and replaced by the farm's own data. The package's labor demand (40–120 person-days/ha/yr) is **far below** the published specialty Central America totals of 181–252 days/ha.

## App implications

1. **Certification catalog as versioned data, not code.** Each scheme gets a `standard_version` with an effective date range: RA v1.3 → v1.4 (1 Oct 2025), C.A.F.E. v4.3, 4C v4.1/v5.0, Fairtrade price table. Every requirement should carry `requirement_id`, `category`, `evidence_types[]` and `record_links[]`.
2. **Fairtrade price table with effective dates.** Rows: `{product: washed_arabica, min_usd_lb: 1.80, from: 2023-08-01, to: 2026-11-30}` and `{2.00, from: 2026-12-01}`, plus premium 0.20 and organic differential 0.40. Contracts look up the price by contract date. Payable price = max(market, minimum) + premium (+ organic). That rule is UNVERIFIED and should be confirmed before shipping.
3. **Multiple deforestation cut-offs per plot.** Store the land-cover evidence per plot and test it against each scheme's date: RA 2014-01-01; EUDR, RA-EUDR and C.A.F.E.-2 2020-12-31; C.A.F.E. 2004-01-01; 4C 2006.
4. **Geometry rules.** Polygon required when the plot is > 4 ha (EUDR) or ≥ 4 ha (RA/4C). Store coordinates at ≥ 6 decimals and in WGS84. Export as GeoJSON or KML. For micro/small producers, allow a postal address as an alternative and flag it.
5. **Pesticide guardrail.** Load the RA prohibited (162) and risk-mitigation (165) lists as versioned reference tables, refreshed in January and July. Block or warn when an application log names a listed active ingredient. The risk-mitigation category triggers checklist items such as PPE and buffers. Drone and aerial applications need non-application-zone checks (10/5 m drone; 30 m aerial, 15 m per riverbank).
6. **Labor module.** Record person-days per activity per block and keep **family labor apart from hired labor**. Allow piece-rate units: cajuela, lata, kg, with a farm-defined kg conversion because the cajuela is a volume unit. Compute kg cherry per person-day and per hour.
7. **Cost KPIs in two views:** with and without imputed family labor (TechnoServe showed about a 50 % difference). Also report labor share %.
8. **Price-realization capture.** Use the STG-aligned fields: contract date, FOB US$/lb green, score, lot size in lb, origin and region, buyer, repeat relationship. Add the C price and differential on the contract date so realized vs reference can be computed. Map scores to the STG bands so farms can benchmark themselves.
9. **Carbon module.** Set the boundary to farm-gate and green, in kg CO2e/kg green. Inputs are N rate and type, fertilizer production factor, wet-mill wastewater treatment and energy. Keep **removals in a separate ledger** from emissions. Only claim removals with a practice-change baseline, monitoring and a reversal flag (Cool Farm 2022; GHG Protocol LSRS v1.1 from 2027). Tag the method (Cool Farm Tool / ISO 14067) on every figure.
10. **Traceability.** Model lots as EPCIS-style events: harvest is an ObjectEvent; drying/milling is a TransformationEvent from cherry lots to green lots; bagging is an AggregationEvent; sale is a TransactionEvent. Keep plot IDs on each event so an EUDR "DDS pack" can be generated: HS 0901, net mass, country/region, plot geolocations, production date range, supplier/customer, and 5-year retention.
