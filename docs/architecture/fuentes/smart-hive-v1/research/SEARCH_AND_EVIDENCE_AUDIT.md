# Search and evidence audit

Search performed 2026-09-16. Purpose: a broad practical evidence map for coffee/cacao pollination experimentation and its software requirements. This is a targeted web literature search and citation-following exercise; no claim of exhaustive database coverage, dual independent screening, PRISMA compliance or a new meta-analysis.

## Search families actually used

- Coffee pollination exclusion, bags, hand cross-pollination, fruit set, final harvest, forest gradients, managed stingless bees, day/night exclusion and coffee quality.
- Cacao/cocoa hand pollination, donor incompatibility, pollen intensity, cherelle wilt, flower visitation, substrate additions, factorial exclusions, cameras and pollen/behavior evidence.
- Roubik coffee branches; Klein arabica/canephora; Ricketts forest valuation; Classen and Martínez-Salinas factorials; Groeneveld, Forbes, Vansynghel, Toledo-Hernández, Merle and Van de Sande cacao work.
- Embedded vision, cacao flower-visitor dataset and exclusion-bag fabric effects.

Sources were publisher articles, PubMed abstracts, author/institutional repositories (including Smithsonian, Embrapa, CATIE, CIRAD, universities), and FAO. Search-index extracts of original article sections were used when direct page fetches were blocked. Secondary aggregators helped discover records but are not the basis for retained experimental findings. No raw experimental data or supplementary datasets were reanalysed.

## Inclusion and limits

Included: directly relevant crop experiments, visitor/landscape studies that inform the field design, methodological studies, an imaging dataset, and selected reviews/handbooks. Reviews are clearly separated from independent field records. Excluded from result extraction: unrelated crops except the shared Bos case-study record and general methods, commercial claims without study evidence, unverified bibliographic mentions and duplicated preprints.

The 40 retained records are 16 coffee, 15 cacao, one cross-crop record, four syntheses/reviews and four methods records. These are organizational categories, not 40 independent yield estimates. Exact sample-size hierarchies, software/model details, equipment part numbers, funding/conflicts and supplementary procedures are incompletely extracted for some papers. `null`/“not extracted” fields disclose this. Further primary-source extraction is required before attempting exact replication.

Publication dating was checked where source filenames or DOI years could mislead: Aristizábal coffee-quality article is volume year 2025, online 2024; Narayanan nocturnal work is volume 2026, online 2025; Ramos stingless-bee work was published January 6, 2026 despite a 2025 DOI suffix. The Xu camera dataset uses the peer-reviewed 2025 publication; the 2024 preprint is not a second record.

## Risk-of-bias and transfer checklist for each future extraction

Record assignment/randomization, independence and clustering, blinding where practical, sham/handling controls, treatment contamination, missing outcomes, endpoint timing, sample-size attrition, genotype/season/region, measurement validity, funding/conflicts and data access. Do not assign an overall “high quality” score merely because an article is peer-reviewed. Abstract-only access is a limitation of this review, not necessarily a flaw in the study.

Check shared datasets before quantitative synthesis: Klein 2003 papers; Brazilian managed-bee papers; related Peruvian cacao studies; Indonesian experiments and later multinational syntheses. Shared authors alone do not prove duplicate data, but overlap must be checked. Meta-analyses may already contain many listed primary studies.
