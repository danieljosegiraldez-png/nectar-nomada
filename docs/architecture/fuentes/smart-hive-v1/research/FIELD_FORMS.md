# Reusable field records

Templates, not recorded experimental data. Blank means not yet entered; use explicit `unknown`, `not measured`, `not applicable` or `missing` when finalizing. Units are required. Store UTC plus local offset and original device time where appropriate. Never place a fabricated example measurement in production records.
