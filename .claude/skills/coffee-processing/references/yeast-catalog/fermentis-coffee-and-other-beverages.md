## MP-72 (outside the table)

SafOeno Bioprotect MP-72, sold by Fermentis ([product page](https://fermentis.com/en/product/safoeno-bioprotect-mp-72/)). The page describes it as a bioprotection yeast, Metschnikowia pulcherrima (not Saccharomyces), for grapes and musts before fermentation: white and rose musts during cold settling, and red grapes during cold soak. Dose 10-20 g/hl (20 g/hl at low temperature or high microbial pressure). Rehydrate in 10x weight water at 25-30 C for 20 min. Applied below 10 C; stored at 4-10 C. It is a wine product, not a coffee product. The page lists no specific seller and points to a "Find a distributor" link.
